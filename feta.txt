**Spinach and feta puff pastry bites**

Makes 12 bites.
Ingredients
- 1 sheet of puff pastry (about 250 g), thawed
- 200 g fresh spinach
- 150 g feta cheese, crumbled
- 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 egg, beaten, for brushing
- Black pepper
- Optional: sesame seeds
Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Heat the olive oil in a pan. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds. Add the spinach and cook until wilted.
3. Let the spinach cool, then squeeze out as much liquid as possible and chop it finely.
4. Mix the spinach with the crumbled feta and a little black pepper.
5. Cut the puff pastry into 12 squares. Place a small spoonful of filling in the centre of each square.
6. Fold each square into a triangle. Press the edges together with a fork to seal.
7. Place the triangles on the baking tray, brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with sesame seeds if desired.
8. Bake for 18–22 minutes, until golden brown and puffed up.
Let them cool for a few minutes before serving.
